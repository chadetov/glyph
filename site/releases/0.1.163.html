--- version: 0.1.163 month: 2026-11 date: 2026-11-22 tags: breaking headline: The stdlib Set, Store and Deque are typed, and an empty deque can't hand back undefined ---

Glyph's own checker could not see the types of set.create, store.create or collections.deque. let n: number = set.create<string>(), seen.add(5) on a Set<string> and seen.nope() all passed check --no-tsc, so the editor, the MCP tools and the applyEdit gate let them through and only tsc caught them. Now set.Set<T>, store.Store<T> and collections.Deque<T> are real types with their members modeled, and those mistakes are E0204, E0211 and E0210.

Three refusals go beyond what tsc does:

  • A match over q.pop_front(), pop_back, peek_front or peek_back with no None arm is E0200. Before, the receiver had no type, both checkers passed it, and an empty deque bound undefined.
  • "${seen}" on one of these types is E0237. It printed [object Object].
  • A record that merely has the same fields is not accepted where a store.Store is declared, and a Store is not accepted where such a record is declared. The same holds for every stdlib type the checker models, decimal.Decimal and url.Url included, and none of them is ever a string, number or bool.

The member tables are checked against the runtime TypeScript in both directions, so adding a method to set.ts without modeling it fails CI.

Two things wait. Writes such as seen.add(x) are typed but not yet held to the mut rule; that is G423 step 2. And a value held by an unannotated module const, such as const HITS = store.create(0), still has no type to the checker until G433.

Breaking

Yes, under full check as well as --no-tsc. The pop_front match without None, the ${seen} interpolation, and the lookalike record in either direction all exit 0 under 0.1.162 and 1 under 0.1.163. The scalar mismatches were already refused by tsc, so they break only under --no-tsc. Across 285 example apps, files, catch cases and test programs, nothing else changed.
